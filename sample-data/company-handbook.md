# Employee Handbook (fictional sample)

## Annual leave

Every full-time employee receives **20 days of paid annual leave per year**.
Leave accrues monthly and may be carried over up to 5 days into the next year.
Part-time employees accrue leave pro rata based on contracted hours.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely **two days per week** with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require HR review and are limited to roles that do
not depend on physical presence (for example, on-site support roles are
excluded).

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. A medical certificate is required
after **three consecutive days** of sick leave. Employees must notify their
manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

## Expense reimbursement

Expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of the purchase date with an
itemized receipt. Reimbursements are processed in the next payroll cycle.

## Security awareness

All employees must complete the annual security awareness training. Access to
the internal knowledge base is provided through the Employee AI Search tool.
